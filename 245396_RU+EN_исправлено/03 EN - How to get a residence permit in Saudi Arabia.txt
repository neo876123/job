<p>Foreigners must move to Saudi Arabia legally to enjoy the Kingdom's full benefits, including advanced infrastructure, high-quality healthcare and excellent education. You can apply for a residence permit based on investment, employment, family ties and education. We will delve into the conditions for obtaining a residence permit in Saudi Arabia for different categories of foreign nationals and examine the specifics of various programs.</p>

<h2 id="id1">Who can obtain a residence permit in Saudi Arabia</h2>
<p>Several categories, including investors, entrepreneurs and professionals over 21, can apply for a residency permit for Saudi Arabia. However, student visas are issued from the age of 17. Investors and entrepreneurs willing to invest in the economy have a high chance of approval. To compare the thresholds with neighbouring countries, see our article on <a href="[:ALIAS_URL:]/faq/how-much-do-you-need-to-invest-in-property-to-get-residency-in-the-gulf-states/">how much to invest in property for residency</a> in the Gulf. Professionals in medical, engineering, and information technology can also apply, mainly if their skills are in high demand. Family members of applicants, including spouses and children, can receive documents alongside the primary applicant.</p>
<p>There are four main types of residence permits in Saudi Arabia:</p>
<ul>
<li>Temporary residency is issued for 1 year and can be renewed or converted to permanent residency upon payment of the required fee;</li>
<li>Permanent residency is an indefinite permit: the fee is paid once, and the status does not need to be renewed every year;</li>
<li>Premium residency is available for buying property worth SAR&nbsp;4&nbsp;million (about USD&nbsp;1.07&nbsp;million) or more;</li>
<li>A work visa (Iqama) is the most economical option for legal residency. It is issued for 1 year during the employment contract, provided the employee maintains their position and meets all program conditions.</li>
</ul>
<p>Since 2024, five more Premium Residency categories have been added for professionals, talents, investors and entrepreneurs. They are described below.</p>
<p>The residence permit for property investment in Saudi Arabia is only available under the premium residency program.</p>

<h2 id="id2">Temporary investment residency</h2>
<p>An investor residence permit in Saudi Arabia through the temporary Premium Residency program requires an investment of SAR 100,000 (approximately USD&nbsp;27,000). This non-refundable fee aims to stimulate economic development. The permit can be extended annually upon repayment of this amount.</p>
<h2 id="id3">Work visa (Iqama)</h2>
<p>An Iqama is the residence permit of a foreign worker in Saudi Arabia: an ID with a personal number that you need to open a bank account, get a phone contract and leave the country. Its digital version is shown in the Absher app.</p>
<p>The Iqama work visa is directly linked to a local sponsor, typically an employer. To obtain an Iqama, a foreign employee must:</p>
<ul>
<li>Sign a contract with a local company and have it certified by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) and the Chamber of Commerce;</li>
<li>Obtain a sponsor letter in the prescribed format, certified by MOFA and the Chamber of Commerce;</li>
<li>Provide educational diplomas and any professional certificates to the Kingdom's Cultural Mission;</li>
<li>Submit a police clearance certificate from their home country;</li>
<li>Undergo a medical examination at approved medical centres;</li>
<li>Apply online;</li>
<li>Attach necessary documents, including a copy of your passport and visa entry information.</li>
</ul>
<p>The processing time for a work visa application is about 3 months, and the outcome depends on the applicant and the sponsor company's reputation.</p>
<p>Since March 2021, workers depend less on their employer. They can issue an exit and re-entry visa themselves through Absher and, once the contract ends, move to another employer without the previous one's consent.</p>
<p>"Workers have more freedom now, but family costs are still on them. Many people only notice the dependants fee at the first Iqama renewal, so it is worth building it into the budget before moving," says a relocation consultant from Riyadh.</p>

<h2 id="id4">Lifetime residency in Saudi Arabia</h2>
<p>Permanent residence in Saudi Arabia can be obtained with a one-time, non-refundable investment of USD&nbsp;213,000 (SAR 800,000) in the country’s economy. The permit is indefinite and does not need to be confirmed every year, provided all programme conditions are met.</p>
<h2 id="id5">Premium residency</h2>
<p>Foreigners can buy property in Saudi Arabia and get residence permit. Purchasing property worth at least SAR 4 million (approximately USD&nbsp;1.07 million) qualifies for premium residency. The property in Saudi Arabia with a residence permit must be fully constructed and ready for occupancy, not under mortgage, and accredited Saudi appraisers must confirm its value. A residence permit in Saudi Arabia by property investment remains valid as long as the owner retains the property. The government fee is SAR&nbsp;4,000 (about USD&nbsp;1,070), paid after approval, plus USD&nbsp;170 for processing.</p>
<p>Since 22 January 2026, the law on property ownership by non-Saudis has been in force: foreigners can buy in zones approved by the government, with Riyadh and Jeddah named first.</p>
<p>"The SAR&nbsp;4&nbsp;million threshold is based on the accredited valuation, not on the price in the contract. If the valuation comes in lower, the property will not qualify for residency, so the valuation is ordered before the deal is signed," notes an immigration consultant from Jeddah.</p>

<h2 id="id6">Cost of residence permit in Saudi Arabia</h2>
<p>The cost of a Saudi residence permit depends on the specific program. The main expenses include the annual residence permit fee of approximately SAR 650 (approximately USD 175) for a work visa, typically covered by the employer. On top of that, the employer pays a monthly levy of SAR&nbsp;800 (about USD&nbsp;213) for each foreign employee. The dependants fee of SAR&nbsp;400 (about USD&nbsp;107) per person per month is usually paid by the worker. Investment residency can cost USD 27,000 annually or a one-time payment of USD 213,000. Obtaining a residence permit in Saudi Arabia by buying property requires USD&nbsp;1.07 million and additional costs. The purchase is subject to the real estate transaction tax of 5% of the price: for a SAR&nbsp;4&nbsp;million property, that is SAR&nbsp;200,000 (about USD&nbsp;53,300). By law, the seller pays it, but contracts often shift the obligation to the buyer.</p>
<p>Medical examinations, translations, and certification of documents such as diplomas, marriage certificates, and birth certificates can cost USD 100 to&nbsp;USD 300.</p>

<h2 id="id7">Advantages of a Saudi Arabian residence permit</h2>
<p>Saudi Arabia, one of the largest countries on the Arabian Peninsula, boasts a developed economy and a stable currency, the Saudi Riyal. What daily life looks like and what <a href="[:ALIAS_URL:]/faq/moving-to-saudi-arabia/">moving to Saudi Arabia</a> costs is covered in a separate article. Foreign nationals with a residence permit can enjoy numerous benefits:</p>
<ul>
<li>Engage in economic activities, including employment and business establishment, and acquire residential and commercial <a href="[:ALIAS_URL:]/property/saudi-arabia/">properties in Saudi Arabia</a>;</li>
<li>No minimum residency requirement for permit holders and their families;</li>
<li>Invite relatives, guests, and foreign workers, with the possibility of obtaining the necessary permits and visas;</li>
<li>Premium Residency holders and their families do not pay the monthly expat and dependants fees.</li>
</ul>
<p>The Kingdom’s tax regime is appealing, as it imposes no personal income or inheritance tax on real estate. Saudi Arabia has agreements in place to avoid double taxation with numerous countries. Saudi Arabia has been a full member of the FATF since 2019 and is not on its grey list, enabling residents to engage in global activities, which is particularly advantageous for business interests in the US and EU markets.</p>

<h2 id="id9">Residence permit application process in Saudi Arabia</h2>
<p>The process starts with choosing the right programme. For a work visa, a job offer from a Saudi employer is necessary, along with meeting qualification requirements. Investment visas necessitate substantial financial contributions to the economy and the acquisition of relevant licenses from Saudi authorities. After selecting the category, foreign nationals must apply for a residence permit in Saudi Arabia and submit all necessary documents through the electronic system or a consulate. Premium Residency applications are accepted by the Premium Residency Center on the pr.gov.sa portal.</p>
<p>Once submitted, the application is reviewed to ensure the applicant meets all criteria. Upon approval, the applicant receives a temporary residence permit, which can later be extended or converted to permanent status, offering more rights and opportunities for living and working.</p>
<h2 id="id10">Documents for obtaining a residence permit in Saudi Arabia</h2>
<p>To legalise residency, only certified documents translated into Arabic are accepted, including:</p>
<ul>
<li>A valid passport from the country of origin; for Premium Residency, it must be valid for at least 6 months;</li>
<li>A police clearance certificate;</li>
<li>A medical report confirming the absence of dangerous diseases;</li>
<li>Financial documentation, including a bank statement or proof of asset ownership in and outside the Kingdom;</li>
<li>Health insurance for the applicant and family members; for Premium Residency, it is kept throughout the validity of the status;</li>
<li>Proof of residence in the Kingdom, such as a rental agreement of an <a href="[:ALIAS_URL:]/property/saudi-arabia/apartments/">apartment in Saudi Arabia</a>.</li>
</ul>
