<p>As the country ranks among the top destinations for work and life opportunities, many foreigners move to Qatar. Foreign citizens are welcome here, and their numbers significantly exceed those of the local population. As a result, the demand for residence permits in the Emirate has grown in recent years. Let's examine the process of obtaining an investor residence permit in Qatar, as well as other types.</p>

<h2 id="id1">Types of residence permits in Qatar</h2>
<p>There are several possible options for obtaining residency in the country:</p>
<ul>
<li>Regardless of the field you choose, it is essential to conclude an employment contract with an employer. The employer, acting as the sponsor, submits the application to the employment service, specifying the gender, nationality, and position of the applicant. The processing time for the request usually ranges from several days to several weeks. Upon receiving a positive response, you can apply to the Ministry of Interior for a visa, the duration of which depends on the type of employment and the term of the contract. After that, you can apply for a residence permit.</li>
<li>Opening a new business or transferring an existing one to the Emirate are viable options. Under Law No. 1 of 2019, a foreigner can own a company in full in most sectors with approval from the Ministry of Commerce and Industry. The exceptions are banking, insurance, natural resource extraction and commercial agencies. Registration and additional documentation are processed for both short-term and long-term visas, allowing you to obtain a residence permit upon completing all necessary procedures.</li>
<li>Since February 2026, entrepreneurs and senior executives can apply for a 10-year residence permit without a sponsor. An entrepreneur needs a recommendation from one of Qatar's business incubators, and an executive is expected to have at least 5 years in top positions. Applications go through the website of the state-owned company Jusour; investment thresholds for this programme have not been officially published.</li>
<li>Purchasing an apartment or <a href="[:ALIAS_URL:]/property/qatar/villas/">house in Qatar</a> worth QAR&nbsp;730,000 (about USD&nbsp;200,000) or more grants the right to residence. However, you can only select a property in a limited number of locations: since June 2026, there are 10 freehold zones and 16 areas where foreigners get a 99-year usufruct right. Before buying <a href="[:ALIAS_URL:]/property/qatar/">property in Qatar</a>, it's worth carefully studying specific areas and their infrastructure. Sources give different validity periods for the resident card, from 1 to 5 years, so it is confirmed when applying. An extension of a residence permit in Qatar is possible an unlimited number of times, provided that property ownership is maintained, and the owner must spend at least 90 days a year in the country. How life in Qatar differs from neighbouring countries and how to plan <a href="[:ALIAS_URL:]/faq/how-to-move-to-live-in-qatar/">moving to Qatar</a> is covered separately on the site.</li>
<li>To obtain a student residence permit, students can enrol in 7 universities, branches of international institutions, and language schools. Universities often provide grants for free education to international students, as well as assistance in obtaining visas and residence permits.</li>
<li>Marriage to a citizenship holder provides an opportunity to obtain a residence permit, but this procedure involves many nuances. The couple must obtain permission from the authorities by passing an interview with the Ministry of Interior committee. With a positive outcome, the application for a residence permit is submitted with the support of the Qatari spouse. A child born to a Qatari parent in marriage automatically receives Qatari citizenship status. However, the local population strictly follows Islamic traditions, which complicates marriages between Muslims and representatives of other religions.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="id2">How to obtain a residence permit in Qatar for investment. How much does it cost</h2>
<p>The minimum cost of a residence permit in Qatar through the property investment programme is QAR&nbsp;730,000 (about USD&nbsp;200,000). A property worth QAR&nbsp;3,650,000 (about USD&nbsp;1&nbsp;million) gives access to the same healthcare, education and certain types of business as permanent residents have. Additional expenses include translation and certification of required documents, payment for a medical examination, and a state fee of USD 825. When the deal is registered, the Ministry of Justice charges a fee of 0.25% of the price, about QAR&nbsp;1,825 (USD&nbsp;500) for a QAR&nbsp;730,000 apartment. To compare Qatar's threshold with neighbouring countries, see an overview of <a href="[:ALIAS_URL:]/faq/how-much-do-you-need-to-invest-in-property-to-get-residency-in-the-gulf-states/">how much to invest in property for residency</a> across the region.</p>
<h2 id="id3">What a residence permit in Qatar provides</h2>
<p>Although a residence permit does not provide foreigners with all the rights and privileges available to citizens of the state, it offers a comfortable life for you and your family:</p>
<ul>
<li>Foreign nationals with residence permits can register with government institutions, such as the labour exchange, and find work or start their own business.</li>
<li>Residents can open accounts and, if necessary, obtain loans from local banks.</li>
<li>The visa provides access to educational and medical services.</li>
<li>Since September 2020, a worker can change employers without the previous employer's permission, giving 1–2 months' notice depending on length of service.</li>
<li>Most workers have not needed their employer's permission to leave the country since 2020.</li>
<li>Since March 2021, a minimum wage of QAR&nbsp;1,000 (about USD&nbsp;275) a month has applied to all workers.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="id5">The process of obtaining a residency permit in Qatar</h2>
<p>It includes several steps:</p>
<ul>
<li>First, you must choose the basis and study the conditions for obtaining a residence permit in Qatar. You can obtain a residence permit based on an employment contract, opening a private business, studying, purchasing a house or <a href="[:ALIAS_URL:]/property/qatar/apartments/">apartment in Qatar</a>, investing in the economy, or marrying a citizen. The country has a sponsorship system, so a sponsor must confirm the visa application. A sponsor can be a spouse, employer, or another organisation willing to vouch for you.</li>
<li>The next stage involves gathering documents, which varies based on the selected option. The state does not recognise the Apostille Convention, so documents such as diplomas or birth certificates must first be translated into English and then certified at a consulate. The chain is: the foreign ministry of the issuing country, the Qatari embassy and, after arrival, Qatar's Ministry of Foreign Affairs.</li>
<li>The application is submitted to the Emirate's migration service either independently or with the assistance of a professional consultant. This step is crucial and necessitates thorough preparation and verification of all required documents.</li>
<li>Obtaining a Qatar ID Card. After successfully submitting and reviewing the application, the applicant will receive an identity card.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="id6">How to get permanent residence in Qatar</h2>
<p>Law No. 10 of 2018 on Permanent Residence regulates the process of obtaining a permanent residence permit. According to its norms, foreign citizens can apply for permanent resident status after an extended period of legal stay in the emirate.</p>
<p>To obtain permanent residency, the following conditions must be met:</p>
<ul>
<li>20 years of residence for foreigners born outside Qatar;</li>
<li>10 years of residence for those born in Qatar.</li>
</ul>
<p>During this time, travel outside the country should not exceed 60 days per year. These days of absence will be deducted from the total period of stay.</p>
<p>Foreign nationals must also meet several additional requirements:</p>
<ul>
<li>The applicant must have a stable income for themselves and their family. The minimum threshold for civil servants is typically around QAR 20,000 per month, equivalent to approximately USD 5,500. For commercial sector workers, the amount is QAR 30,000 (approximately USD 8,250);</li>
<li>The applicant must confirm the absence of criminal records or other offences;</li>
<li>It is necessary to demonstrate a sufficient level of Arabic language.</li>
</ul>
<p>Foreign nationals whose activities are of particular interest to Qatar or who have made significant contributions to the country's development can apply for permanent residency, regardless of the duration of their stay in the country. Buying a home worth QAR&nbsp;3,650,000 does not move an applicant into this category: it brings permanent-residency benefits, but not the status itself. Permanent residency is also capped at 100 people a year.</p>

<h2 id="id9">Qatar residence rules: what matters as of November 2026</h2>
<p>Information checked on 9 November 2026. Two changes were added this year. In February, a 10-year residence permit without a sponsor appeared for entrepreneurs and senior executives, and in June, Cabinet Decision No. 21 expanded the list of ownership zones for foreigners to ten, adding the Simaisma resort. The thresholds for home buyers, QAR&nbsp;730,000 and QAR&nbsp;3,650,000, are unchanged, as are the labour rules of 2020–2021.</p>
<h2 id="id7">Documents for obtaining a residence permit in Qatar</h2>
<p>The exact list of documents to apply for a residence permit in Qatar depends on the specific programme. Still, there is a basic package required for all:</p>
<ul>
<li>passport (valid for at least 6 months);</li>
<li>photographs;</li>
<li>biometrics;</li>
<li>certified copies of diplomas, certificates, and a contract with the employer for a work visa;</li>
<li>marriage certificate and children's birth certificates for a family visa;</li>
<li>medical examination (tests for HIV, syphilis, hepatitis B and C, fluorography);</li>
<li>medical insurance.</li>
</ul>
<p>Documents must be translated into Arabic or English and legalised: notarisation alone is not enough. The package can be submitted independently or through an employer, attaching an employment contract and a receipt for payment of the state fee. The application is typically reviewed within 14 days; however, the process may take up to 2 months if additional information is required.</p>
