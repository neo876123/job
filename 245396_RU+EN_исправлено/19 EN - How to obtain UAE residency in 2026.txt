<h2 id="id2">Types of investor visas in the UAE</h2>

Foreign nationals can obtain residency through investments in real estate or by establishing a business. Obtaining a UAE Golden Visa is one of the most common options among expats, as it allows staying in the country for an extended period (from 5 years, or 10 years for property). Buyers of apartments and <a href="[:ALIAS_URL:]/property/uae/dubai/villas/">villas in Dubai</a> can participate in a separate program. Its investment threshold is lower, but the validity period for this residency category is 2 years.[/quote]</pre>
<h3>UAE Golden Visa</h3>
<p>This long-term residence permit enables foreign nationals to reside, work, or study in the country. Real estate purchase is one of the grounds for its issuance. The main advantages of this option include the following:</p>
<ul>
<li>long validity period – 5 or 10 years;</li>
<li>possibility of extension;</li>
<li>no need for a sponsor;</li>
<li>ability to stay outside the country for more than 6 months while maintaining status;</li>
<li>ability to sponsor family members, including spouses and children of any age (in case of the sponsor's death, they can remain in the country until the expiration of their residence permit);</li>
<li>ability to sponsor an unlimited number of domestic workers.</li>
</ul>
<p>Investors must purchase one or more properties worth at least AED 2 million (USD&nbsp;544,500). A mortgage is no obstacle: in 2026, Dubai's authorities look at the certified value of the property rather than the amount paid, and they accept off-plan homes with a registered contract on the same basis. The status is granted for 10 years. For a comparison with the neighbours — Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman and Bahrain — see a breakdown of <a href="[:ALIAS_URL:]/faq/how-much-do-you-need-to-invest-in-property-to-get-residency-in-the-gulf-states/">how much to invest in property for residency</a> in each of these countries.</p>
<p>Entrepreneurs ready to invest in an economic project based on technical innovations and willing to take on risk can also apply for a 5-year UAE residence permit by investment. They must provide an approval letter from:</p>
<ul>
<li>a local auditor confirming that the project value is at least AED 500,000 (USD&nbsp;136,000);</li>
<li>the emirate authorities stating that the project has a technical focus or is related to innovative technologies;</li>
<li>an accredited business incubator.</li>
</ul>
<p>Investments in a fund allow obtaining a 10-year investment visa in UAE under the following conditions:</p>
<ul>
<li>providing a letter from a fund accredited in the country stating that the investor has a deposit of 2 million AED (USD&nbsp;544,500) or a valid commercial/industrial license and articles of association stating that the investor's capital is at least AED 2 million (USD&nbsp;544,500);</li>
<li>submitting a letter from the Federal Tax Authority stating that the investor pays at least AED 250,000 (USD&nbsp;68,000) per year to the state.</li>
</ul>
<h3>UAE resident visa by real estate purchase</h3>
<p>The Golden Residency is designed for buyers of freehold properties in any emirate. There is also a separate program for investors in residential properties, such as houses and <a href="[:ALIAS_URL:]/property/uae/dubai/apartments/">apartments in Dubai</a>. It allows legalisation for 2 years through the acquisition of a property worth AED 750,000 (USD&nbsp;204,000). Along with the principal applicant, their spouse, unmarried daughters of any age and unmarried sons under 25 can apply for this residency category.</p>
<h3>UAE Green Visa</h3>
<p>A residence permit is issued to foreign nationals for a period of 5 years. Investments in a new or existing business in the country are among the grounds for applying. The minimum investment threshold is AED 1 million (USD&nbsp;272,500). Residents can extend their status and include spouses, unmarried daughters of any age and sons under 25 in the application.</p>
<p>"The Green Visa is often underrated. For a freelancer earning AED&nbsp;360,000 a year, it gives the same 5 years without a sponsor as the Golden Visa for an innovation project, but without investing in a business. The key is that the income must be proven for two consecutive years," says a visa programme specialist in Abu Dhabi.</p>
<p>Following the permit cancellation, a six-month grace period is provided for an extension. Citizens and residents do not have to sponsor investors when obtaining an investor visa in the UAE.</p>

<h2 id="id3">Other ways of obtaining a UAE residence visa</h2>
<p>"In addition to investments in real estate and business, there are many other ways to obtain a residence permit. Foreign nationals can legalise their stay by signing an employment contract with a local company, enrolling in an accredited educational institution, and confirming the availability of pension contributions, regular income from remote employment, family ties with residents, or outstanding talents," says Oleg Samoilov.</p>
<h3>Through employment</h3>
<p>Foreign nationals who have signed an employment contract with a local company are eligible to apply for a work residence permit. In this case, the employer handles the process. They apply to the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratization or a free economic zone, depending on the type of legal entity.</p>
<p>If approved, the company receives an entry permit for the employee. Then, the foreigner arrives in the country and gets an arrival stamp. After the information is transmitted to the Immigration Service, they undergo a medical examination and submit biometric data. If the applicant fulfills all conditions and confirms compliance with the requirements, they receive a residence visa for employees in the UAE within a few weeks.</p>
<p>Qualified specialists can also apply for a 5-year Green Visa. For this, they must meet the following criteria:</p>
<ul>
<li>having a valid employment contract with a local employer;</li>
<li>the required level of professional qualification meeting the requirements of the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratization;</li>
<li>a bachelor's degree or equivalent;</li>
<li>salary – from AED 15,000 (USD&nbsp;4,100) per month.</li>
</ul>
<h3>For freelancers</h3>
<p>Freelancers and self-employed individuals can also apply for a 5-year Green residency. For this, they need to provide:</p>
<ul>
<li>freelance/self-employment permit from the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratization;</li>
<li>confirmation of a bachelor's or specialist degree;</li>
<li>confirmation of income from individual labour activity for the previous 2 years in the amount of at least AED 360,000 (USD&nbsp;98,000) per year or financial solvency throughout the stay in the country.</li>
</ul>
<p>Additionally, foreign nationals can obtain a one-year virtual work permit. A freelancer visa in UAE is designed for specialists working abroad in web design, information and media technologies, education, etc. Applicants are required to confirm their remote employment and monthly income, starting from USD 5,000 for Dubai and USD 3,500 for other emirates.</p>

<h3>Studying at accredited universities</h3>
<p>An international student can remain in the country to receive education with sponsorship support from either their parent, who is a resident or from an accredited university or college in which they study.</p>
<p>Important notes:</p>
<ul>
<li>Student residence permits are issued for 1 year, with an extension for a similar period, upon providing proof of continued education from the institution where the foreigner is enrolled.</li>
<li>High achievers who have completed high school with a grade of at least 95% or university with a GPA of 3.75 can apply for a 5-year Golden residence permit without a sponsor, provided they submit a recommendation letter from the Ministry of Education.</li>

<p>Residents can sponsor their sons only until they reach the age of 25. Male students older than this age can remain under their parents' sponsorship if they provide proof of attending a higher education institution for at least 1 year. Unmarried daughters can be sponsored regardless of their age.</p>
<p>To apply, one must provide a certificate issued by the educational institution indicating the duration of the study, an IELTS or TOEFL certificate confirming the level of English proficiency and proof of having sufficient funds to live in the country. A student permit grants the right to employment, with limitations on working hours.</p>
<h3>Program for retirees</h3>
<p>Foreign nationals can apply for a retiree visa in the UAE. Individuals who have worked in the Arab Emirates or abroad for at least 15 years or who were 55 years of age or older at the time of retirement are eligible to apply. They must also meet one of the following requirements:</p>
<ul>
<li>ownership of one or more properties worth at least AED 1 million (USD&nbsp;272,500);</li>
<li>savings of at least AED 1 million (USD&nbsp;272,500);</li>
<li>monthly income of at least AED 15,000 (USD&nbsp;4,100) for living in Dubai or AED 20,000 (USD&nbsp;5,500) for living in any other emirate.</li>
</ul>
<p>The retirement residence permit is valid for a period of 5 years. Residents can renew their status if they meet the criteria for its initial receipt.</p>
<h3>For family members</h3>
<p>Foreign nationals can sponsor their families' residence in the country with a valid residence permit. Their minimum salary must be at least AED 4,000 (USD&nbsp;1,100) per month or AED 3,000 (USD&nbsp;820) per month with housing provided. For legalisation, adult family members need to undergo a medical examination.</p>
<p>Important notes:</p>
<ul>
<li>a mother can sponsor her children in exceptional cases approved by the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security;</li>
<li>a resident sponsor has 60 days to apply for a residence permit for dependents after they visit the country on an entry permit;</li>
<li>a resident can sponsor their parents, and their permit will be issued on an annual basis regardless of the sponsor's residence permit validity period;</li>
<li>the type of profession is no longer a condition for a foreign worker to determine the possibility of sponsoring their family members.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="id6">Possible reasons for UAE residence visa refusal</h2>
<ul>
<li>Non-compliance with the requirements of the residence permit category. Refusal is possible if a foreigner, for example, has insufficient income to obtain a freelancer visa.</li>
<li>Problems with documents. Errors in completing the application form, providing false information, or failing to submit necessary documents can result in the rejection of the application.</li>
<li>Poor credit history or debts. If the applicant has debts, financial violations or unfulfilled obligations to banks, this can be grounds for refusal.</li>
<li>Criminal history. A criminal record, especially for financial crimes, fraud, violence or other serious offenses, can lead to application rejection.</li>
<li>Previous violation of visa regime. Deportation, overstaying, or being refused entry in the past can negatively impact obtaining a residence permit.</li>
<li>Health problems. Detection of serious diseases such as tuberculosis or HIV during a medical examination can be a reason for refusal.</li>
<li>Security considerations. If the applicant is associated with activities that threaten the national security of the state (such as terrorism or extremism), they will be denied residency.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="id7">Rights and obligations of UAE residents</h2>
<p>A residence permit in the United Arab Emirates allows foreign nationals to reside legally, open bank accounts of any type, obtain a driver's license and access medical services and education. They can freely move within the Emirates and travel abroad without needing to obtain a new permit upon return.</p>
<p>Residents' obligations include compliance with local legislation and respect for Islamic traditions and culture. It is essential to renew your status promptly, as violating the migration regime can result in fines and deportation. They must pay taxes (if applicable), comply with the terms of the employment contract, and refrain from engaging in activities contrary to the norms of public behaviour.</p>
<p>Foreign nationals may lose their residence permits for serious offenses, such as fraud, making inappropriate social media posts or violating Sharia law.</p>

<h2 id="id8">How to renew residence permit in UAE</h2>
<p>The extension of the residence permit depends on its category. For example, foreign nationals who have legalised through the purchase of real estate in Dubai or other emirates must confirm that the property remains in their ownership. Mandatory stages for extending residency include a medical examination, updating the Emirates ID, renewing the insurance policy, and submitting an application to the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security or the General Directorate of Residency and Foreign Affairs.</p>
<p>An application for a residence permit in UAE with a list of necessary documents, including a passport and a 3x4 cm color photo, can also be submitted online. The review period ranges from a few days to 2 weeks, so the status extension process must begin before the current residence permit expires.</p>
