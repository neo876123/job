<p>When moving to the UAE, for example, to Dubai, it is quite difficult to choose housing because of the many properties on offer. First of all, you need to decide what you are looking for, whether it’s a house or an apartment. To understand which <a href="../property/dubai/">property in Dubai</a> is better for purchase, you need to compare both options and assess your needs.</p>
<p>Permanent residence as many people understand it is not available in the UAE: foreign residents hold visas that need renewing — an employment visa, a five-year retirement visa or a ten-year Golden Visa. The type of home makes no difference to the visa. The Dubai Land Department (DLD) issues a 10-year Golden Visa to the owner of an apartment or villa priced at AED 2 million ($544,600) and above. Since April 29, 2026, the 2-year property owner visa has had no minimum price if the home is finished and registered fully in the applicant’s name; in a joint purchase, every co-owner must hold a stake of AED 400,000 ($108,900) or more.</p>

<h2 id="id1">Types of Real Estate and their suitability</h2>
<p>Urban lifestyle enthusiasts or professionals who need to live close to the workplace often choose <a href="../property/dubai/apartments/">apartments in Dubai</a> for permanent residence.</p>
<p>Families with children, pensioners, and people who live more secluded lives and desire more freedom prefer spacious houses.</p>
<p>Between the two sits the townhouse: a home in a row of adjoining units, with a separate entrance and a little yard. Dubai market reports track its prices and yields separately from villas.</p>
<h2 id="id2">Demand Trends</h2>
<p>In the May 2022 report of the real estate consulting company Asteco, villas and houses remained the leading demand. Switching to remote work during the coronavirus pandemic led tenants to look for more spacious spaces, which increased the demand for villas in the suburbs. As a result, rental rates in Arabian Ranches and Dubai Hills rose by 47% and 48% year-on-year, respectively. In 2022, the focus was still on houses. A limited number of offers increased rental rates and accelerated the process of buying real estate.</p>
<p>The annual rental growth in the Dubai villa market in the first half of 2022 averaged 25%, while the average apartment rental rates increased by 14%.</p>
<p>Prices also grew in 2022, especially in the villa segment. The average quarterly increase in value was 5%, and the average annual increase was 40%. The high demand for villas led to a rise in apartment sales, which increased in the first quarter of 2022 by 5% compared to the fourth quarter of 2021 and by 22% compared to the first quarter of 2021. A separate overview of <a href="../faq/types-of-real-estate-what-apartments-and-houses-are-there-in-dubai/">property types in Dubai</a> explains how a townhouse differs from a villa and what other home formats the emirate offers.</p>
<h3>Demand for Apartments and Villas in 2026</h3>
<p>According to Property Monitor, 33,969 residential deals were registered in Dubai in Q3 2026. Apartments accounted for 87.6% of them, townhouses for 9.3% and villas for 3.1%. Villas are more often bought ready: 624 resale deals against 431 off-plan. For apartments and townhouses, off-plan deals are the majority.</p>
<p>A year earlier, houses had a larger share. By betterhomes’ count based on DLD data, villas and townhouses made up about 18% of deals in 2025 and about 40% of their total value — AED 221 billion ($60.2 billion) out of AED 547 billion.</p>
<p>The drop came mainly from off-plan houses. According to Cavendish Maxwell, villas and townhouses made up close to 15% of off-plan sales in H1 2026 (almost 24% a year earlier), while their share of the resale market rose from 17% to 20%.</p>
<p>Purchase prices fell over the year. In ValuStrat’s valuation-based index, villa values dropped 4.8% and apartment values 7.2% between October 2025 and October 2026. In October, the index gained 0.1% month on month after a cumulative 10.2% decline since February.</p>
<p>The REIDIN index, built on median prices per m², shows a milder picture: over the same year, apartments lost 2.16% and villas gained 0.52%. The two companies use different methods, so calculations should stick to one index.</p>
<p>Rents are falling for both formats in 2026. By the REIDIN index, apartment rents slipped 7.2% and villa rents 6.7% in the year through October 2026, and the decline sped up toward the fall.</p>
<p>Cavendish Maxwell reaches the opposite result: an average rent of AED 77.3 per sq ft a year (≈AED 832 per m²) in September 2026, +5.5% on September 2025. The company counts all contracts, including renewals, which explains the gap. The two sets of figures should be read separately.</p>

<h3>Space</h3>
<p>Houses are more spacious than apartments. Unlike the latter, they include a plot of land, a private garden, a swimming pool, a children's playground, and private parking, making them advantageous for families with children and pets.</p>
<p>When moving into a flat, you have to face limitations regarding space, privacy, and parking spaces.</p>
<h3>Return on Investment</h3>
<p>Depending on the area, the return on investment in houses and apartments may differ. Flats are more profitable, with an average return of 6.96% per year (Property Monitor, November 2026). The yield of the villas varies from 3.22% in Jumeirah Islands to 7.45% in Al Barari per year, with an average of 4.67%; townhouses average 5.20%.</p>
<p>These are the areas of Dubai with the highest apartment yields, according to Property Monitor for November 2026:</p>
<ul>
<li>International City – 8.94%;</li>
<li>Dubai Silicon Oasis – 8.11%;</li>
<li>Dubai Sports City – 8.04%;</li>
<li>Jumeirah Village Circle – 7.53%;</li>
<li>Arjan – 7.48%.</li>
</ul>
<p>Of the 2022 list, only International City remains in the top five. Property Monitor has no fresh estimates for Barsha Heights, The Greens or Dubai Studio City: it calculates yields for areas popular with investors, not for the whole city.</p>
<p>Dubai's most profitable districts with detached houses, based on the same data:</p>

<h3>Prices for Houses and Apartments in Dubai</h3>
<p>Prices for villas are higher on average than for flats.</p>
<p>Fresh per-property averages come from ValuStrat, which works from valuations. In August 2026, it valued the typical Dubai apartment at AED 1.782 million ($485,200) and the typical villa at AED 12.969 million ($3.53 million).</p>
<p>Between communities, villa and townhouse prices per sq ft differ several times over. According to Property Monitor (average deal price, October 2026), it was AED 907 (≈AED 9,760 per m²) in DAMAC Hills 2, AED 1,536 in Mudon, AED 2,352 in Arabian Ranches, AED 3,311 in Dubai Hills Estate and AED 5,821 (≈AED 62,660 per m²) on Palm Jumeirah.</p>

<h3>Atmosphere</h3>
<p>Houses have more space and are closer to nature. The air in cottage settlements is cleaner, and they are far from any noise pollution. You will have a free-standing house hidden inside a garden and away from the hustle and bustle of the city, with all the benefits of living in a community. Flats cannot provide you with the same level of privacy and freedom.</p>
<p>[quote]“Families often buy a villa in the first months after moving, before they know what the area is like in summer or how long the drive to work takes. I advise renting the type of home you are considering for the first year and buying after that. Rents are falling in 2026, so a trial year costs less than it did a couple of years ago,” says a residential property consultant for families relocating to the UAE.[/quote]</p>
