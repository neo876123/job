<p>Sharjah is one of the new sought-after UAE destinations for investment in apartments and villas. The emirate borders on Dubai, has access to the Persian and Oman Gulfs, and boasts excellent resort infrastructure and lower cost of living than in Dubai. The area is popular for both investing in real estate and comfortable living. Al Khan, Al Nahda and Muwaileh are among the most popular spots to buy a home in Sharjah.</p>
<p>In January–June 2026, 59,460 property transactions worth a combined AED 29.5 billion ($8.03 billion) passed through the Sharjah Real Estate Registration Department. Their number was 23.7% higher than a year before. Buyers held passports of 121 countries.</p>

<h2 id="id1">Al Khan</h2>
<p>Al Khan is a resort region located in the southern part of Sharjah, on the shores of the lagoon of the same name. The main attractions of the area are the Sharjah Aquarium, home to giant manta rays, and the Maritime Museum, where you can see traditional Arabian dhows. In addition, Al Khan houses the Al Arab Mall, a medical clinic, supermarkets, numerous beachfront cafes and restaurants.</p>
<p>The local 600-meter long sandy Al Khan Beach is one of the most popular coasts in Sharjah. There are sports grounds, a mobile library and various types of water activities, including kayaking, kite surfing, parasailing, and many other options.</p>
<p>On June 16, 2026, the Ruler of Sharjah announced an island project in Al Khan Lagoon, between the lagoon entrance and Maryam Island. It will be reached by an underwater tunnel, with a large car park beneath the island and restaurants, shops and a theater on top. No timeline has been given yet.</p>
<h3>Why is it profitable to buy apartments in Al Khan?</h3>
<p>Al Khan is a promising area for buying a home in Sharjah. Among the main advantages of the location are:</p>
<ul>
<li>convenient location within 20 minutes’ drive from the center of Dubai;</li>
<li>a road program toward Dubai: the first phase of the Al Taawun tunnel (three lanes in each direction) is scheduled to open in November 2026 and the full tunnel in February 2027, and the program also covers interchanges at Al Khan;</li>
<li>proximity to the best sandy beaches of the city;</li>
<li>well-developed infrastructure with schools, medical facilities, shops, food outlets and much more in the area;</li>
<li>high liquidity of housing thanks to the location by the sea (1,077 deals worth about AED 1.3 billion, or $354 million, were registered here in the first half of 2026, the third-highest figure among the emirate's areas);</li>
<li>an opportunity to purchase an apartment or a house with a discount from the developer at the construction stage and pay for the property in instalments;</li>
<li>in some new residential complexes there are freehold apartments, which means that foreigners can purchase real estate and be rightful owners – under Law No. 2 of 2022, only in projects approved for foreign buyers.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Al Khan Housing Market Trends</h3>
<p>Today new residential complexes are being erected in Al Khan. One of them is the beachfront community of Maryam Island by Eagle Hills. Here, nationals of other countries can buy freehold housing – that is how the developer and the portals market it, while the emirate has no published official list of approved projects. On the Bayut index, Al Khan apartments were advertised at AED 887 per sq ft on average in October 2026 (roughly AED 9,550 per m², or $2,600), which is considerably cheaper than similar <a href="../property/dubai/">properties in Dubai</a>.</p>
<p>Over 12 months, the average asking price in the area fell by 11.95%; six months earlier, the index stood at AED 923 per sq ft. Rents moved the other way, rising 6.82% over the same year to AED 43 per sq ft annually. Savills links the decline in Sharjah apartment prices to a large volume of new homes reaching the market, especially in waterfront projects.</p>
<p>Al Khan is located on the seafront, which makes it attractive for tourists and expats. This, in turn, opens up good prospects for investors who want to securely invest in real estate ensuring rental income.</p>
<p>The rent rates for a 1-bedroom apartment are AED 38,000 ($10,350) a year – the average in Bayut's annual report for 2025, down 12.7% from a year earlier. The return on investment for properties in Al Khan is 3.88% a year for apartments, according to the same report (gross yield). Average asking prices were AED 699,000 ($190,300) for a 1-bedroom, AED 1,043,000 ($284,000) for a 2-bedroom and AED 1,562,000 ($425,300) for a 3-bedroom. The portal names Al Khan the most sought-after area in Sharjah among apartment buyers.</p>
<h2 id="id2">Al Nahda</h2>
<p>The small residential area of Al Nahda is located along Al Ittihad Road (E11, the continuation of Dubai's Sheikh Zayed Road), a major highway linking Sharjah and Dubai. An important part of the residential community is the central park with a football field, a basketball court, jogging tracks, children’s playgrounds and leisure areas. In addition, Al Nahda features the MK Sport complex, fitness clubs, several medical clinics, supermarkets, Al Nahda Gifts Center, IMall, schools and nurseries.</p>
<p>Al Nahda is known for its abundance of cafes and restaurants. Here you can find venues for any taste, including Indian and Chinese restaurants. Children will love Adventureland, an amusement park with jungle-themed rides, located on the edge of the area.</p>
<p>Al Nahda in Sharjah runs right up to the Dubai neighborhood of the same name. Sahara Centre mall sits directly on the border between the two emirates.</p>
<h3>Al Nahda Property Market Trends</h3>
<p>Until 2022, real estate in Al Nahda with full ownership was sold only to UAE nationals, while foreigners could buy leasehold property – a usufruct for a maximum of 100 years. Law No. 2 of 2022 opened unlimited-term ownership to foreigners, but only in approved projects. Freehold for foreign buyers in Al Nahda is not confirmed by open sources. That is why it makes sense to buy here only in approved projects, once the Sharjah Real Estate Registration Department has confirmed the building's status. Nevertheless, Al Nahda is attractive for investment thanks to affordable prices and good quality of residential complexes.</p>
<p>One can consider off-plan apartments at a bargain price from the developer. In addition, you can pay in interest-free installments. In 2025, Al Nahda was one of the 11 areas where the department registered new projects, 14 in total. Public reports do not give the names of the projects in the area.</p>
<p>Bayut's annual report for 2025 puts the average ask for a 1-bedroom apartment in Al Nahda at AED 470,000 ($128,000), lower than in the emirate's other popular areas. Sellers asked AED 690,000 ($187,900) for a 2-bedroom and AED 1,191,000 ($324,300) for a 3-bedroom. The price per sq ft rose 21% over the year. Annual rents: a studio at AED 29,000 ($7,900), a 1-bedroom at AED 37,000 ($10,080), and a 2-bedroom at AED 48,000 ($13,070), each up 14% on 2024.</p>
<p>[quote]“Most of my calls come from people who work in Dubai and want to pay less for housing. Before a viewing, I find out two things: how long the client is ready to sit in morning traffic, and whether they really need ownership. In Al Nahda, the second question is settled building by building rather than for the whole area, so I request the documents for the building in advance,” says a sales and leasing agent in Al Nahda, Sharjah.[/quote]</p>
<h3>Why buy property in Al Nahda?</h3>
<p>Al Nahda is a 10-15-minute drive from the sea and 20 minutes from Downtown Dubai. This provides residents with convenient access to the beach and entertainment infrastructure of the neighboring Al Khan resort community. And if necessary, you can quickly get to Dubai, where large financial, business and shopping centers and popular attractions in the UAE are concentrated.</p>
<p>Al Nahda has a well-developed social infrastructure and an urban transport system, which makes the area a great option for a comfortable lifestyle. An important advantage of Al Nahda is the fact that despite the convenient location near the seacoast, the cost of modern real estate here is much lower than in <a href="../new-developments/dubai-emirate/dubai/">new projects in Dubai</a>. Also, rental rates for housing are lower, which attracts potential tenants to Al Nahda.</p>
<p>Investors can purchase buy-to-let housing in Al Nahda, as the ROI here is kept at a considerably high level. For example, Bayut's annual report for 2025 estimates that apartments in the area yield 7.06% gross. This is a figure before costs: building service charges, the agent's fee and empty months between tenants still come out of the rent.</p>
<h2 id="id3">Muwaileh</h2>
<p>Muwaileh is a large community located in the outskirts of Sharjah, close to Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed Road (E311) and a 20-minute drive from Sharjah Beach. This quiet urban community is ideal for a family lifestyle. It is home to various educational institutions, including the prestigious private school GEMS Cambridge International. The region has a well-developed infrastructure. There are medical clinics, supermarkets, public parks, restaurants and cafes, and city bus transportation.</p>
<p>University City, with the University of Sharjah and the American University of Sharjah, is close by, and Aljada and Al Zahia are next door. Besides regular routes, the area has Bus on Demand: residents book a bus through an app in Muwaileh Commercial, Aljada, University City and other areas.</p>
<p>In October 2026, the Sharjah Real Estate Registration Department opened a branch in Muwaileh. It registers sales and mortgages, issues title deeds, carries out valuations and attests contracts. The head of the department linked the opening to the fast growth of construction and population in Muwaileh and nearby areas.</p>
<h3>Muwaileh Property Market Trends</h3>
<p>The Muwaileh real estate market has both move-in ready housing and off-plan projects, where prices are lower than for new <a href="../property/dubai/apartments/">apartments in Dubai</a>. In some communities, foreigners are allowed to buy property. For example, the developer Alef Group sells Al Mamsha as freehold for all nationalities, and in neighboring Aljada foreign ownership is officially confirmed.</p>
<p>In Bayut's annual report for 2025, sellers in Muwaileh asked on average AED 731,000 ($199,000) for a 1-bedroom apartment, AED 1,138,000 ($309,900) for a 2-bedroom and AED 1,453,000 ($395,600) for a 3-bedroom. A 3-bedroom villa cost about AED 1.93 million ($525,500), while the price per sq ft of villas fell 4.5% over the year.</p>
<p>Rental rates for housing in Muwaileh are also lower than for similar apartments and <a href="../property/dubai/villas/">villas in Dubai</a>. Per the same Bayut report, annual rent in the area averages AED 43,000 ($11,710) for a 1-bedroom apartment, AED 26,000 ($7,080) for a studio and AED 58,000 ($15,790) for a 2-bedroom. A 3-bedroom villa brings in about AED 142,000 ($38,670) a year.</p>
<p>Construction in the area goes on. In June 2025, Alef Group broke ground on Al Mamsha Raseel, 2,210 units worth AED 1.1 billion ($299.5 million), with handover set for December 2028. In January 2026, the company and Emaar Hospitality launched Palace Residences Al Mamsha.</p>
<p>In neighboring Aljada, the developer Arada started selling The Gate 6 in March 2026: 220 apartments with one to four bedrooms from AED 970,000 ($264,100). The payment plan is 5% on booking, 30% over two years and 65% on handover. Jenna followed in April, with 631 apartments in six buildings and handover at the end of Q1 2029.</p>
<h3>Why is it profitable to buy apartments and villas in Muwaileh?</h3>
<p>The main advantage of buying property in Muwaileh is affordable prices together with top-quality housing. The area has a peaceful atmosphere of a countryside lifestyle, and most of the complexes are low-rise and mid-rise projects.</p>
<p>Muwaileh is popular with those who work in the nearby industrial areas. Investors can consider a buy-to-let apartment or a house, as affordable housing prices stimulate demand among tenants. According to Bayut's annual report for 2025, it is the most sought-after area in Sharjah among apartment tenants.</p>
<p>In the first half of 2026, Muwaileh Commercial ranked first in the emirate by both the number and the value of deals: 2,385 transactions worth AED 2.8 billion ($762 million).</p>
<p>Investors who plan to invest in Muwaileh rental housing for a long term can expect an annual return of about 4.98% for apartments and 5.78% for villas – the gross yields given in Bayut's annual report for 2025.</p>

<h2 id="id5">Buying rules in Sharjah for foreigners</h2>
<p>Foreign ownership in Sharjah was allowed by Law No. 2 of 2022. On November 1, 2022, the emirate's Executive Council clarified that nationals of all countries may own property of any type with no time limit, but only where the areas and projects have been approved. Before that, a foreigner could only get a usufruct capped at 100 years.</p>
<p>As of July 2026, 50 projects had been approved, 6 of them in the first half of 2026. There is no complete list in the public domain.</p>
<p>The fee for registering a deal is 4% of the price; who pays it is up to the contract. In January 2026, the government fee was cut by half for buyers at the ACRES exhibition as a one-off offer.</p>
<p>Steps for a foreign buyer:</p>
<ol>
<li>Ask the Sharjah Real Estate Registration Department whether the project is approved for foreigners.</li>
<li>Check what right the contract grants: unlimited-term ownership or long-term use.</li>
<li>Budget for the 4% fee: on an apartment priced at AED 700,000 ($190,600), that is AED 28,000 ($7,620).</li>
<li>Register the deal and get the title deed at the department. Buyers in Muwaileh and nearby areas can use the new branch opened in October 2026.</li>
</ol>

<h2 id="id1">Al Khan</h2>
<p>Al Khan находится в южной части Шарджи. Это курортный регион, расположенный на берегу одноименной лагуны. <h3>Тенденции рынка жилья в Al Khan</h3>
<p>Сегодня в Al Khan ведется строительство новых жилых комплексов. Один из них — это прибрежное комьюнити Maryam Island от застройщика Eagle Hills. Здесь жилье можно купить в частную собственность гражданам любых стран — так проект продают застройщик и порталы, а официального списка одобренных проектов эмират не выкладывает. По индексу Bayut, в октябре 2026 года квартиры в Al Khan стоили в объявлениях в среднем 887 AED за фут² (это примерно 9550 AED за м², или 2600 $), что заметно дешевле, чем аналогичная <a href="[:ALIAS_URL:]/property/dubai/">недвижимость в Дубае</a>.</p>
<p>За 12 месяцев средняя цена в объявлениях района снизилась на 11,95%: полгода назад индекс стоял на 923 AED за фут². Фрихолд в Al Nahda по открытым данным не подтверждён, поэтому статус дома до задатка проверяют через департамент, который регистрирует сделки в эмирате.
[/item]
[item question="Почему квартиры в Al Khan дешевеют, а аренда дорожает?"]По индексу Bayut за год к октябрю 2026 года цены объявлений в районе снизились на 11,95%, а ставки аренды выросли на 6,82%. Savills объясняет снижение цен большим объёмом нового жилья, особенно в прибрежных проектах. При этом спрос, по оценке компании, сильнее всего держится в проектах на побережье от Al Khan до Al Mamzar.
[/item]
[item question="Во что обойдётся регистрация покупки?"]Сбор составляет 4% от цены. Для квартиры в Al Khan с 1 спальней по средней цене объявлений Bayut за 2025 год — 699 000 AED (190 300 $) — это 27 960 AED (7610 $). Кто платит сбор, прописывают в договоре.
[/item]
[item question="Где оформить сделку, если квартира в Muwaileh?"]С октября 2026 года в районе работает филиал Департамента регистрации недвижимости Шарджи. Там регистрируют куплю-продажу и ипотеку, выдают свидетельства о собственности, проводят оценку и заверяют договоры.
[/item]
[item question="Может ли арендодатель поднять плату в первые годы?"]По описанию Bayut, закон Шарджи № 5/2024 не разрешает повышать аренду в первые три года, если стороны не договорились иначе. Текст закона стоит прочитать до подписания договора, а доход на этот срок считать по стартовой ставке.
[/item]
